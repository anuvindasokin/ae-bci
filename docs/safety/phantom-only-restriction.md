# Phantom-Only Restriction

AE-BCI is an early-stage experimental research platform.

All current designs, procedures and source settings are intended exclusively for
conductive saline tanks, synthetic tissue-equivalent phantoms, synthetic skulls
and appropriately controlled ex-vivo materials.

The system must not be connected to, implanted in, positioned on or tested with a
person or animal.

Any future biological research would require independent review by qualified
researchers, institutional ethics approval, validated acoustic exposure limits,
electrical safety review, thermal and cavitation assessment, sterile and
biocompatible hardware, medical oversight and compliance with applicable regulations.
