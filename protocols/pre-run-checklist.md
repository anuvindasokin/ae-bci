# Pre-Run Checklist

- [ ] Non-living material and approved scope verified
- [ ] Enclosures, isolation, current limits, loads, coupling, and containment checked
- [ ] Thermal sensors, power monitoring, overloads, and emergency stops tested
- [ ] Cable, shielding, grounding, source, receiver, and acoustic geometry photographed
- [ ] Metadata, raw storage, backups, and clocks active
- [ ] Baseline acquisition accepted before energising combined condition
