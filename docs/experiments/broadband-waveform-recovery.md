# Broadband Waveform Recovery

Recover band-limited sine, multitone, sweeps, pulses, synthetic neural-like, and
permitted recorded waveforms across 10 Hz–8 kHz. Stream X/Y at ≥25 kS/s (preferably
40–50 kS/s or higher) with provisional 12–20 kHz output bandwidth. Compare signed
waveform, spectrum, phase, delay, correlation, distortion, and noise.
