# Requirements

Provide galvanic isolation, battery or isolated power, bipolar output, near-zero
DC offset, adjustable hardware-limited current, compliance monitoring, calibrated
current sense, reactive-load stability, and usable 10 Hz–8 kHz bandwidth with
preferred 5 Hz–10 kHz design margin. Current range, electrode load, compliance,
stability criteria, isolation rating, and parts are `TBD`; no final schematic is
created until verified.
