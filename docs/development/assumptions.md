# Assumptions Register

Current working assumptions are a small-signal conductivity model, stable medium
and geometry during a run, coherent carrier/reference clocks, adequate sampling,
time-invariant calibrated transfer functions, SI-unit metadata, and separable
control conditions. The 500 kHz configuration is only a baseline; higher frequency
is not assumed superior. No transducer, instrument, pressure, safety limit, hardware
performance, acoustoelectric response, spatial resolution, biological effect, or
clinical utility is assumed validated.
