# Shutdown Checklist

- [ ] Stop source current, RF output, and stage motion
- [ ] Verify stored energy discharged and temperatures safe
- [ ] Inspect equipment, fixture, coupling, electrodes, and containment
- [ ] Record end state, deviations, overloads, alarms, and incidents
- [ ] Close raw files, preserve immutable copy, verify hashes, and back up
- [ ] Link logs, metadata, calibrations, results, and exclusions
