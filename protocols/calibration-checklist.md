# Calibration Checklist

- [ ] Source current/frequency and electrode impedance
- [ ] Receiver impedance, cable, transfer, CMRR, and noise
- [ ] Transducer impedance, RF power path, hydrophone, field, and temperature
- [ ] Lock-in amplitude, phase, reference, filters, rate, range, and overload
- [ ] Coordinate registration, clocks, environment, uncertainties, and expiry
