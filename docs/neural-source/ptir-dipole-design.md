# PtIr Dipole Design

The preliminary source uses Pt90/Ir10 or equivalent, 0.20–0.25 mm wire, 0.5–1 mm
exposed tips, approximately 3 mm spacing, and PTFE, PFA, or parylene insulation.
These are proposed values, not a fabricated design. Dipole moment is `p = I × d`.
Supplier and exact geometry are `TBD`.
