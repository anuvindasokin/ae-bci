# Security and Safety Reporting

Do not publish exploit details, personal data, credentials, hazardous operating
parameters, or sensitive facility information in a public issue. Privately report
software vulnerabilities and equipment-safety concerns to the repository owner;
the dedicated contact and response SLA are `TBD`.

This policy does not authorise human or animal use. Immediately stop work after an
unexpected electrical, RF, acoustic, thermal, mechanical, or containment event.
